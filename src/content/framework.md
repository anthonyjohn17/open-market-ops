# Liquidity intelligence framework

Five steps form a complete opportunity loop:

1. **Feed** — messy public data before consensus pricing  
2. **Asset** — mispriced or latent value in the signal  
3. **Trigger** — urgency (why now)  
4. **Buyer** — economic counterparty with budget and authority  
5. **Monetization** — liquidity mechanism (flip, broker, retainer, lead-gen, data product)

Compressed: **Signal → Spread → Match → Monetize.**

MVP implements Feed, Asset, Trigger, and Scoring; Buyer and Monetization are workflow stubs until v0.2 engines ship.
